Confirm you’re 18 or older

To show mature content you first need a quick live check. It takes about 30 seconds and you only do it once.

Mature content notice

Some content on Nova is only for adults and may be explicit. Until the check is passed, no mature videos, images, thumbnails, titles or previews are loaded — and “Hide Mature Content” stays on.

How it works

  • Centre your face in the oval, in good light.
  • Do 4 quick random moves — such as a blink, a head turn, a wink or a smile — one at a time.
  • Each move has a circle: orange while you do it, green when done, red if it failed.

Your camera & privacy

  • The camera is on only during the check and switches off the moment it ends or you cancel.
  • Movement is analysed on your device. Nothing is recorded or saved.
  • At the end, one still frame is sent securely to our age-check service and is not kept. We store only a yes/no “18+ verified” flag. We don’t identify you or read emotions.
